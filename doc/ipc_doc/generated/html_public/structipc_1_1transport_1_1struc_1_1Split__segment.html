size_t ipc::transport::struc::Split_segment::m_n_cont_subsegs

How many elements immediately following the one at m_start_idx (but not including it!) compose the pre-split segment.

This value shall be greater than or equal to 1. (0 would mean the segment was split into 1 components, and by our convention in that degenerate case the Split_segment shall not be generated at all.)
