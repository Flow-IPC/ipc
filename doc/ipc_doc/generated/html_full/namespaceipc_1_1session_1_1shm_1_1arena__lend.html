using ipc::session::shm::arena_lend::Borrower_pool_stats_list = typedef std::vector<std::unique_ptr<ipc::shm::arena_lend::stat::Borrower_pool_stats> >

Canonical short-hand for a list of per-arena stat::Borrower_pool_stats, as produced by the borrower-side process-wide stat accessor (see jemalloc::Shm_session::borrower_pool_stats_process_wide()).

The unique_ptr wrapper allows cheap moves and lets the stat-sets – not natively copyable due to their atomic members – live in a vector.
