Detailed Description

Internal-use thread-local singleton-style access to a kj::AsyncIoContext which, for each given thread, shall exist only as long as ref-counted handles to it (presumably held by Ez_rpc_servers and Ez_rpc_clients) remain outstanding.

So just call this_thread_obj() and hold on to the returned shared_ptr handle, accessing ->m_kj_io off it – from that same thread only – and let the ref-counted group expire once no longer needed.

Impl notes

While the concept of what is happening here I lifted from capnp::EzRpc* internals, I (ygoldfel) did it it in the vanilla C++1x way: use weak_ptr (instead of the KJ way: kj::Own and kj::Refcounted). Plus I removed a couple wordy things such as the accessors (public access to m_kj_io seems fine, in this internal-use situation).

thread_local boost::weak_ptr< Ez_rpc_kj_io > ipc::transport::struc::shm::rpc::Ez_rpc_kj_io::s_this_thread_obj_observer
staticprivate

Weak-pointer observer to the last-retured in this thread (via this_thread_obj()) Ptr; or null.

Effectively it is in one of two states:

  • If s_this_thread_obj_observer.lock() == nullptr: this_thread_obj() has not been called; or it has been called, but the returned Ptr group has become empty (no references remain).
  • Else: The Ptr group returned by the last this_thread_obj() is alive.
