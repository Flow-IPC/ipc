33/**
34 * Our transport::asio_local_stream_socket utilities support transmitting at most this many native handles (FDs)
35 * per single-message OS-call or batch-transmission per-message slot.
36 *
37 * ### Rationale: Current value ###
38 * It is 1. transport::asio_local_stream_socket utilities, while themselves opportunistically public, exist
39 * in the first place to support sync_io::Native_socket_stream and derivative(s) which, formally speaking,
40 * themselves implement concepts sync_io::Native_handle_sender and sync_io::Native_handle_receiver. Those
41 * APIs as of this writing permit at most 1 `Native_handle` to be transmitted per message.
42 *
43 * That could all change at some point; but for now this straightforwardly need only be 1.
44 */
45constexpr size_t N_PAYLOAD_FDS = 1;
