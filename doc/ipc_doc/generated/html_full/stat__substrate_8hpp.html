namespace  ipc::shm::arena_lend
 ipc::shm sub-module providing the SHM-provider family based on the arena-lending paradigm: each process allocates in its own arena(s), lending the resulting objects to other processes, which can read them but not allocate (nor deallocate) in that arena.
